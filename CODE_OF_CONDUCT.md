# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while discussing ideas.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner using a contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, and information will be shared only as needed to address the concern.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents a `genegith` project in public spaces.
